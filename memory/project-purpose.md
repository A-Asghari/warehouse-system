WarehouseSystem is the in-house warehouse and production management app, used day to day
by warehouse staff and production-stage staff. It pulls item, stock and BOM data from Sepidar.

**Why:** Tracks per-stage production and stock movement that Sepidar itself does not model.

**How to apply:** Changes affect real staff on the LAN; test on a scratch copy (see CLAUDE.md)
before touching the live db.json.
